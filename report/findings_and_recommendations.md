## 🎯 Targeted Marketing Strategy Playbook

1. **Champions / High-Value VIPs**:
   - Deliver white-glove concierge support, early access to new catalog drops, and exclusive tiered loyalty privileges. Avoid mass discounts.
2. **Loyal Core Customers**:
   - Deploy cross-selling triggers based on purchase history and establish milestone anniversary rewards.
3. **Recent / Potential Loyalists**:
   - Automated onboarding email drip campaigns offering a 10% coupon on the second order within 14 days.
4. **At-Risk / Hibernating**:
   - Launch automated 'We Miss You' win-back campaigns and feedback surveys with a 15% reactivation incentive.
